About

Accessibility is a runtime property.

I am a software engineer focused on how assistive technology actually behaves, not on how the markup is supposed to behave.

I have spent ten years building software and the last five working on accessibility inside product teams. At EY I was a Senior Frontend Engineer and Accessibility Lead from 2021 to 2025. Since 2025 I have worked as a freelance engineer, including a multi-tenant CRM with AI-assisted replies for Mercado Libre sellers.

My open source work is mostly in design systems: twelve merged pull requests across Adobe React Spectrum, IBM Carbon, IBM Products, Meta Astryx, MUI Base UI, MUI Material UI and shadcn/ui. The fixes are usually small and structural: an ARIA contract, a focus path, an announcement that competes with the one the platform already makes.

That is also the thesis behind Preflight: a linter can tell you whether the attributes are present. It cannot tell you what a blind person hears when they use the component. The second question requires running the page, in a browser, with assistive technology attached.

I live in Buenos Aires and work remotely. I write in English, and occasionally in Spanish for the local market.
