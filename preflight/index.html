Preflight

Your AI built the site. This proves it works, and tells the builder how to fix it.

A pre-launch accessibility gate that reads your page the way a screen reader consumes it, then hands back a repair prompt your AI builder can execute.

What it shows you

Every attribute a linter checks can be present and the page can still be unusable. Preflight walks the page and records what is announced, in order, including the gaps:

  1  document
  2  image                        <- an image with no name
  3  link, Pricing
  4  button                       <- a button with no name
  5  heading, Ship your landing page in minutes, level 1
  6  image                        <- another one

How it works

  1. The transcript

    The page is walked with a screen reader and every phrase it speaks is recorded, in order. A role announced with no name is marked and explained in plain language.

  2. The verdict

    One score that refuses to be green while someone is blocked. A page with 92 findings fixed and one unusable checkout does not get an A.

  3. The fix prompt

    An ordered prompt, ready to paste into the tool that generated the page. Every step names the element, the change, and the announcement the reader must produce afterwards.

It does not certify anything, it does not crawl, and it does not edit your code. Automated checks catch roughly a third of accessibility defects; every report says so.
